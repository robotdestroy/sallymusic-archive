# Links and Press

Original SallyMusic.com content recovered from the Internet Archive. Dates below are **capture dates, not publication dates**. Exact text/link duplicates are grouped; differing versions are retained. Site navigation, decorative images and shopping form controls are omitted. Original spelling and wording are retained except that third-party review quotations are omitted from this Markdown edition; reviewer names, publication links and the band’s own commentary remain. No external full review articles were retrieved or reproduced.

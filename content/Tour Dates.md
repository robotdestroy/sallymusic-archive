# Tour Dates

Sally's gigography: all **70 entries** preserved in the archived SallyMusic.com show history, from April 19, 2003 through November 19, 2007.

## Sources and transcription notes

- [SallyMusic.com gigography — July 15, 2011 Wayback snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20110715232633/http://sallymusic.com/cms/?itemid=102)
- [SallyMusic.com tour-dates page — September 29, 2007 Wayback snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20070929161501/http://sallymusic.com/cms/?itemid=102)
- The two snapshots contain the same 69 earlier gigography entries. The 2011 snapshot also includes November 19, 2007 in the gigography; the other snapshot contains it as a separate show listing, with the time, price, and bill preserved below.
- **Date conflict retained:** July 23, 2006 is listed for both Reversible Eye Gallery in Chicago and Velvet Lounge in Washington, D.C. The archive does not resolve this apparent conflict; neither date has been silently changed.
- **Source spelling retained:** the March 29, 2007 venue is written “Darkoom,” whereas the August 10, 2006 venue is written “Darkroom.”
- **Non-show placeholder excluded from the gigography:** the page's playful “Mars” header changes from September 9, 2009 to December 21, 2012 between snapshots. Its accompanying text reads “it'll be lunacy,” “the outer realms of the universe,” “30022 Quasar Street, Moonsville, WY 99999,” and “start time eight post meridian.” It is not treated as a real performance.

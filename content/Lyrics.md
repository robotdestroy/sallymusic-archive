## Version 3 — secret.html — captured 2004-08-30

- [Archived source (20040830185728)](https://web.archive.org/web/20040830185728/http://sallymusic.com:80/secret.html)
- [Archived source (20041013060427)](https://web.archive.org/web/20041013060427/http://sallymusic.com:80/secret.html)
- [Archived source (20041223064123)](https://web.archive.org/web/20041223064123/http://sallymusic.com:80/secret.html)

**sally - god answered my speed**  
  
1. [easy breezy street](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/01easybreezystreet.mp3)  
a b-side. nothing but guitars contrary to popular belief.  
  
2. [watermelon](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/02watermelon.mp3) (demo)  
demo for the album track watermelon. i like the creepy singing in the beginning.  
  
3. [superior nut](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/03superiornut.mp3) (rehearsal)  
from a rehearsal. the guitar sounds bad ass here. i wish we could have gotten guitars on the album that sound this mean.  
  
4. [the castle of wales](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/04thecastleofwales.mp3) (demo)  
we open with this song live on occasion and it has become the opening of the song 'silver, is grey.'  
this is a hopped up version of it. it's much slower and moodier when we play it together.  
  
5. [the coming spring](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/05thecomingspring.mp3) (demo)  
acoustic demo of the coming spring. i like the crispness of the guitar coming on and off.  
  
6. [sebring me home](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/06sebringmehome.mp3)  
b-side. referencing the chrysler sebring.  
  
7. [run from the cops](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/07runfromthecops.mp3) (demo)  
demo for the album track. i used to like this version better. but now i hate both of them.  
  
8. [knife in my hip bone, pain](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/08knifeinmyhipbonepain.mp3)  
a b-side. featuring harmonies.  
  
9. [your helpful hints](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/09yourhelpfulhints.mp3) (demo)  
if the album version is skyy vodka, this version is heavy tranquilizers.  
  
10. [sedate me](http://sallymusic.com/godansweredmyspeed/10sedateme.mp3) (studio version)

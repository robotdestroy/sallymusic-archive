### 04/03 — the love box ain't ready yet.

*Original date label: 04/03 (month/day; year not printed). CMS item 19. Posted by: robotdestroy.*

a crazy chain-smoking german contacted us from an underground bunker in berlin. he is putting this and that together to make this and that. it takes time to make this and that.

we sat in an above ground bunker for far too long and created something that is far too short to believe. our four song extended play disc is done, the title remains the same : the attrition e.p. i can't let you in on all the secrets, but you can see a little [film](http://sallymusic.com/video/attritionwebFINALhigh.mov) we made in 1969, shot on super 8mm in the dry-docks of nebraska. if you need assisted internetting, you can vidi a low-fi version [here](http://sallymusic.com/video/attritionwebFINALmed.mov).

if you find your way up to the irritable crow, he will maybe play you a song or two that you can't even pay a soul to buy yet. he's a nice crow, the german named [karsten](http://roboteralarm.de/) brought him to us from a crow farm. he's a nice german.

we are the rubberband band. wait til you see the dubious amount of shows you can see us play in the next months. and we might even travel a bit around the country in the summer solstice. keep that in mind for your summer plans, you non-midwest sinners.

the last paragraph contained questionable use of the word dubious. but as you can see, according to merriam-webster, my official source of linguistics, dubious means : "b : questionable or suspect as to true nature or quality." touche.

back to the noise machine. keep your heads on straight.

07/29 — the german built the love machine.

Original date label: 07/29 (month/day; year not printed). CMS item 117. Posted by: roboteralarm.

partake, berate. from the splendor tree of dissonance, the wretched crow spews terrible news. the attrition e.p. is a consumable good. the price is five-gali pence. you desire and retire by walking over to our new and improved sally-mart. if you do it correctly, it will be shipped by the united states postal service to you on august eleventh, in the year of our bored two-thousand-five. oh fear the coming winter by picking up a storm cloud enhanced cloak while you are in town.

speaking of your town, we might very well be in it, broken footed and paranoid. sally, the collective, is riding the oregon trail in a carriage called 'the elbow, and secret societies tour.' check our two-our section for explicit details.

and alas alak, the worst of the despicable horrors. on august eleventh two-thousand-five, our backbone and medical man hunter morris will play his finale with us. as he has been extradited to our fatherland and home country. no more will he speak the wonders of wiki-knowledge. celebrate his independence as well as our record release as well as our homecoming (we bring riddles from lands beyond) on august eleventh. check the war-plans for all the important logistics.

whilst you adore the unadorable, take a look around. we live in a temporary autonomous zone and we wanted to share it with you. find spectacular secrets in hidden corners. with that i must return my dagger to my pocket and open my trusty bottle of terror-noise, and take a strong redolent whif

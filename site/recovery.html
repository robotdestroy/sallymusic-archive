Recovery notes and limitations

Recovered on 30 October 2026 from Internet Archive captures of SallyMusic.com. This is a reading archive of recovered original site content, not a certification that every historical post survives.

News coverage

  • 49 distinct historical news/CMS pages were fetched successfully: ten differing homepage captures, individual entry captures, static news.html and news2003.html revisions, /old/ mirrors, and two archive-index pages.
  • The static news archive contains 48 posts across 47 printed publication dates, September 2003–December 2004. Two separate posts share 21 January 2004. The 15 September 2003 post has an earlier longer version and a later shorter version; both remain.
  • CMS news contains 26 distinct item IDs. Item 201 has two recovered body versions, both retained. News.md therefore contains 76 body texts for approximately 74 posts (including image-only entries).
  • CMS homepages label entries month/day; individual-entry pages use day/month. Neither recovered template prints the publication year. News.md preserves those labels with their format stated and does not substitute capture years for publication years.
  • CMS item numbers are not consecutive. They were shared with other content, including shows; gaps do not establish a count of missing news posts.
  • The CMS monthly-archive pages returned a heading but no month links. Historical homepage versions and surviving individual pages were used instead. No unarchived database rows can be reconstructed from templates alone.
  • Original site prose, spelling, capitalization and links are retained. HTML entities and whitespace are normalized for Markdown. Link-only host variations (www, port 80) and mirrored /old/ paths are ignored when identifying otherwise identical posts. The original URLs can be checked in the linked archived source pages; raw HTML is not included in this Obsidian copy.
  • Decorative navigation/footer images are omitted. News-body images are recorded as image links, not transcribed. Some entries are entirely image-based; their visible text is not recovered in this written archive.

Early landing-page captures were also inspected through 2003 (earliest 4 June 2003); they contained navigation rather than additional news prose.

Other sections and boundaries

Biographies, release information, lyrics, audio/media descriptions, merchandise text and the site's links/press section were recovered separately. Versions are labeled by capture date, not presumed publication date. Full site biographies are historical, sometimes satirical material—not independently verified factual accounts. The files note that context. Third-party review quotations are omitted; review links and attributions remain.

Recovered navigation includes news, audio, pictures, biography, discography, shows, mailing list and contact. No separately labeled “do” section was identified. The earlier photo area was called “pretentious.” The later main site used Flash; HTML wrappers do not guarantee recovery of text contained inside Flash assets. Newsletter/contact forms, login interfaces, image galleries without substantive text, and unrelated later domain content are outside this written archive. Tour dates were already recovered separately and have not been duplicated here.

The old site also invited readers to a LiveJournal account for more comprehensive news, including friends-only entries. That external/private material was not accessed or reconstructed. The archived invitation is evidence of another possible gap, not evidence that the missing posts are publicly recoverable.

Evidence and reproducibility

  • Each Markdown entry or section carries an Internet Archive source link.
  • This Obsidian delivery contains the nine Markdown notes and their source links only.
  • The recovery working files—raw HTML, paired provenance JSON, capture inventories, source/extraction records and rebuilding scripts—are not included here.
  • Inline raw: filenames in some notes are identifiers retained from the recovery process, not local file links or attachments.
  • Captures should be identified by their linked resolved timestamps. A requested capture date can differ from the actual resolved date; one initial discovery request labeled home2005, for example, resolved to February 2006.

Archive link timestamps are UTC capture timestamps, not dates on which the band published or performed anything. Old outbound links may be dead, moved or now belong to someone else; their preservation is not an endorsement of present destinations.

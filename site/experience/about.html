Welcome back to Dismay.

The main SallyMusic.com experience really was Flash. This reconstruction uses the original cutout artwork, stage proportions, section layouts and navigation in ordinary HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It needs no Flash plugin or emulator.

What survives

The saved site contains nine primary Flash 7 movies: the loader, an optional intro, the main scene, and audio, pictures, biography, tour, merchandise and links sections. They use a 1007 × 675 stage at 31 frames per second. The saved HTML wrapper recommended Flash Player 7 and a 1024 × 768 screen, and offered the CMS as a low-bandwidth HTML alternative.

The Flash wrapper is preserved in a 17 December 2005 capture. The exact launch date remains unknown. Earlier 2003–2004 HTML pages also survive. The three saved loader, intro and main SWFs have SHA-1 digests matching their public Wayback records: loader, 2006, intro, 2011, main, 2011. Capture dates do not establish creation or publication dates.

What was rebuilt

ElementReconstruction
Collage and labelsOriginal bitmap and vector artwork rendered from the saved movies, retaining the original stage coordinates.
Main entranceThe original main timeline’s frames 2–83, converted to a browser image animation and played at 75% of the source’s 31 fps. This is distinct from the optional 01_intro.swf movie.
Flying menuSix original human-headed bugs, with original wing frames. The source’s 630 × 430 movement bounds and 5–14 pixels-per-frame velocity range were ported to JavaScript. Random paths vary; exact mouse springs and secondary reactions are approximations.
NavigationThe original handwritten strip and seven destinations: news, audio, pictures, bio, tour, merch and links. Keyboard and small-screen navigation are additions.
MusicTen original Sally Radio MP3 files, the original illustrated controls, and browser audio. The missing Flash playlist’s exact order is unknown. The readable playlist below the collage is an addition. Audio starts only after a click.
Writing and picturesRecovered writing fills real HTML scrolling regions. The original dynamic CMS feeds are missing. Earlier biographies, links and merchandise are labeled; original gallery album order is unknown.

Limits and deliberate changes

This is a reconstruction, not an emulated copy of the entire ActionScript program. The paper transitions and speaker movement are approximations. Small secondary animations, the optional intro movie, original speech-bubble reactions and moving gallery eyes have not all been ported. Some secondary sprite art is sampled from a timeline frame.

The six gallery pictures come from the saved band website files and were visually reviewed. Their grid order and the accessible photograph dialog are additions. The old newsletter, contact popups, comments, login and purchases remain inactive. There are no requests to the old backend, trackers or external runtime dependencies. Old outbound links open Wayback.

On small screens the original artwork scales as one scene, while recovered writing and controls appear below at a readable size. Motion can be paused; reduced-motion preferences are respected. The optional bird ambience is the original exported sound and is off until enabled.

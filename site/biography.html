Biography of sally by C. Mitchell Turner

The group sprung out of the collaborations of notedly prolific singer/songwriter Charlie Deets.
Almost born into music, his mother had worked in the rich Chicago radio scene during the hay-day
of musical exploration, and dated many of rock's greatest: Keith Richards, Mick Jagger, Paul Simon,
and J.C. Hooke of the Cryan' Shames. It is highly unlikely that Charlie was spawned from any of
these suitors. Instead, he was born the son of a printer.

As an only child, most of Charlie's youth was spent playing alone. It was only natural that he'd find
freedom and expression through music. A self taught guitarist, he had knocked around in many bands,
fitting in none. While away at college in Bloomington, Illinois, Charlie found himself fronting the
experimental pop fusion outfit, the borderlinejet. His popularity caught the eye of art student named
Melissa Neis and within a few months the two found themselves spending all there time together,
talking to no one else.

Missy was the sort of child that only a mother could love. The messy girl with tangled hair, wiping mud
and snot on her frilly expensive dress; the sort of girl you wouldn't want to have touching anything
of yours. Maybe she just had a different view of life, but her rebellious filth was just the sort of
escape that fulfilled the neurotic and frightened Charlie.

Sadly, the borderlinejet's, first ep failed miserably, and the band's popularity slowly faded from
central Illinois. Charlie's pre-existing fears from childhood spiraled out of control and consequentially
his grievous abuse of tranquilizers did as well. Strung out, paranoid, and angry, Charlie blamed
everyone around him for his failures. The band split, and so did the university and Charlie.
Broken, he quit making his music entirely and returned to Chicago only to find himself immersed in
his old lifestyle with old friends with self-destructive problems of their own.

Unwilling to sink into the gutter he packed up his worldly belongings and moved to Los Angeles.
To make ends meet Charlie worked in a recording studio that primary dealt in gangsta rap artists.
A life-changing event occurred in late April of '00. After an artistic disagreement in the studio,
a notorious producer held a firearm to Charlie's temple, handed him an acoustic guitar and told
him to play. What happened next could only be called divine intervention. Pilled out on Xanax,
Charlie calmly slid the guitar to the floor and shattered it with his foot, fortunately for him the
pistol was not loaded, and his job as an engineer came to a close.

With nowhere to turn he locked himself in his white one room apartment above Hollywood Blvd. With a
pawn shop guitar, a refrigerator full of Lunchables, and some Christmas lights, the next three months
became a lonely blur. The only musical output to come from his time in L.A. is the enigmatic
Peter Piper L.P., a collection of forgettable and opiated guitar driven drone songs with the occasional
scream or howl from Charlie. It also featured snippets of spoken word from the personal micro-cassette
taped ramblings of the controversial Actor/Philosopher Ben Dixon.

On a positive note, Missy had never given up hope on Charlie. She waited for him back in Illinois,
unwilling to give up the dream that one day the two of them could be happy together. But, like all things
in this world, nothing turns out as planned. Her psychotic babbling and endless drinking had become far
too much for Charlie, who had returned penniless to Chicago, only to take up residence on the floor of
alcoholic Screenwriter C. Mitchell Turner's apartment. The sort of life lived there proved far too much
of a strain on his frail mental state and Missy was at her wits end with the constant unfaithfulness,
the paranoid delusions, and the cavalcade of ruined teenage girls who surrounded Charlie's debouched life.
Finally, after a sickening visit from Missy, a distant Charlie notified Missy to never speak to him again.
The destructive bout had not only left Charlie homeless, but loveless as well. Finally, after years of madness,
Charlie sought help and two and a half years slipped by.

  1. With plenty of free time, Charlie had started writing song with astonishing intensity. He created
    two full length cds, the Flagrent Foul L.P. and the Dilated L.P. by himself, giving
    copies to his family and friends. He was extremely nervous about playing in public, but as
    attention grew towards his songwriting, he started to feel comfortable with music again.

Ready to play out, Charlie searched out a new band. Coming up empty handed around the scene,
Charlie was becoming disillusioned. It was a strange choice then, when after years of silence,
Charlie invited Missy to play music with him. Being an amateur guitar player, and naturally
adept at plucking at things, Missy picked up bass quickly. The two both understood that there
would be no love between them ever again.

Charlie knew all too well that he needed to make music; it was the only thing that gave him purpose.
If he was going to keep his sanity in this confusing world, than he had to follow his heart.
The band began to search out a drummer. Fortunately, C. Mitchell felt remorse about throwing
Charlie out, so as a consolation, he introduced him to his cousin.

Jeff Shafar, an amateur snowboarder/longboarder/photographer, was known for his endless reserves
of energy and the uncanny ability to do anything. Jeff started playing drums with the band and
the connection was made immediately. It is necessary to mention that showbiz is nothing new for Jeff,
he has danced in the Nutcracker for 12 consecutive years with the Salt Creek Ballet. He's also
performed in countless other productions from New York to Seattle. Swan Lake, Aaron Copeland's
Billy the Kid, and Stravinsky's Firebird Suite, just to name a few.

The band played out for the better half of 2003, but not without much trouble. It was not
uncommon to have a band member missing from a show. Charlie grew more and more increasingly
perturbed with the situation. The band decided to commit some music to record, and in
October of 2003, the band entered Gravity Studios to record with former skateboarder
Doug McBride at the helm. Paribus Records was the label kind enough to
sponsor the endeavor.

And with that, this is sally, with their first ep, our very first extended play.

  • C. Mitchell Turner

Biography of sally by C. Mitchell Turner

It reminds me of Bob Seger. There's this line that you hear uttered over and over again by faded
women in dive bars. The line is from Against the Wind, but they never remember, they sure as shit
know it's Bob Seger, but they never get the song. Fire Inside? Like a Rock? Roll Me Away? I wish.

I've known old Deets for neigh on 15 years now, the last time I saw him it was late maybe 1 or 2
in the AM. Not that late for me but he's got some full time corporate middle manager shit runnin'
these days. It was a Wednesday, I'd been shooting pool with a very drunk Puerto Rican man, who's
a good friend of mine, but he wouldn't give Baby back to me until I had beaten him 10 games,
shooting for drinks. So, I make it to Deets' house late and drunk. Charlie was all wild-eyed
and excited in a secret way; the way an average man is excited about water bondage. I was laying
in his car contemplating Henessey, when he hurridly produced a bottle of herbal laxative and two
table spoons. Immediately, I understood all too well and eagerly, thus we engulfed several
spoonfuls and he started driving. Now, much has been made about the sleeping pill races in my days,
but this was horrifying and productive. The stuff is basically liquid clay and it bonds to all
the evil inside you and then forces it out. For me evil constitutes sausages burgers prime rib, for
Charlie evil is like tofu and carrot sticks. I don't know what he was hoping to get out, but we
drove and the feeling came, and we struggled to hold it in.

There are only two things that govern Charlie's being these days; the memories of Weathermen,
News anchors, and aging adult rockers, with their soft yet forceful fingers, and the Cleanliness
INSIDE. Think about it, everything you consume. It stays. Elvis, John Wayne, do you know
how much extra weight they were dragging to the finish line within their colons? John Wayne
had about 40 pounds and the King of Rock 'n' Roll was toting around a 9 year old boy in his colon!
And they found him on the shitter. HOT DAMN!!!! Well, needless to say Charlie Deets ain't going
out like that. Oh, no. He stays clean. You might not understand. Few people do. There is a
completely blissful euphoria when it's gone from you; release, catharsis, rebirth, BLISS.

You ever take sedatives for a long time? Comes a point where you realize you've been on vacation
for days, maybe weeks, months, and you just don't know how you got to where you're at. You rub
your eyes, adjust to the lights, take a look around and there's this skinny little vegan man with
an arm on his back beating liberals, less liberal than himself, over the head with Gideon's Book,
expounding on his supported assertation that "Faggots will burn in hell." You wonder how to make
him stop pounding on the bathroom door, screaming about Satanism, and the dire fate of homosexuals.
Take another slug of Pellegrino out of a paper bag. It's not his house, those aren't his opinions
(so you hope), and it's not his party. People are getting confused, angry, somehow you know that
you've got to get him out of that hellhole before all the New Wave hooker girls and Art Institute
Jello punks lynch him in a courtyard off Wolcott. After all, this man is the lead guitarist of
your band, a recording genius, and he's from Delaware. He can't die here, not like this. Wake up
Charlie, this is your life; the agitation, the Veganism, the Nihilsm, you love this man, admit it.
You'd love to punch him in the teeth all the same. Give him a fishing pole and fish sandwich and
he'll taunt the poor vagrants until the cows come home or until "they get a fucking job." All I
know is that there ain't nobody in this world I'd trust more to crack a bottle over my head than
the very righteous Mr. Mark Berlin.

So, I'm sitting in Gunther Murphey's talking to this REALLY skinny dude. I mean the corpse of
Charlie wishes he could get this skinny, dude has some super skinny legs. I'm thinking: "Dude,
you play elevator music. I heard the samples. Don't tell me that's fucking Jazz, I may be musically
illiterate, but that's not Jazz, that's sucking Kenny G's dick. Maybe, I'll give you Wynton Marsalis,
but I don't relish the thought of having the cock in my mouth. And that's not JAZZ. MAN. Art Blakey,
Sunny Murray. Them's JAZZ. You = Elevator music." Yeah, he's a vegan too, but he sure 'nuff puts
down the booze. Must not be opposed to meat for health reasons, cause he drinks like he wants to die.
He got the indy rock t-shirts, make the indy chicks swoon, cause he's really really skinny and
that's what them broads dig on. Them Le Tigre chicks, they get all hot when they can make out 9
different bone formations in the wrist alone. They must all be on heroin. I swear I'll never
understand this world. EAT SOMETHING PLEASE! But, I guess you don't wanna be one of those uber
fat McDonalds people, eh? FOOD IS FUEL! Yeah, so, at any rate, I notice that Hunter, (yeah, Hunter
Morris. I'm pretty sure that's his real name, ladies. I don't think he made it up to sound famous
or nothing.) he's got himself a double kicker, well, I didn't wanna assume anything before I heard.
But, get him angry, Elevator Jazz be damned, shades of early Andy Whale, dare I speak it, Jesper
Stromblad?!!!??!! Brutal, they should feed him herbal laxatives and white cross before every show.
Yeah, despite the whole elevator misunderstanding, dude's pretty Rite. Judge a book by it's cover
and all. He's got a farm of robots that he builds, not like models, but I guess they actually do
things for him. I suppose that eventually they'll all conspire and off him, ala. Tales from the
Crypt. But, oh well, at least he's seen the world, spent some time in Japan, I don't think he
really speaks the language as fluently as I do, and the only proof of his stay is this picture I
seen of him in front of a pagoda with a little girls umbrella. I'm all fine with him and stuff,
but I got this sneaking suspicion that he's wantin' to take a poke at my lady.

So, Milk and Toast, kicked Missy to the curb, like the gristle off a porterhouse. It couldn't
have come at a better time, because yours truly happens to be of eligible bachelor standing,
FINALLY, after all this time, I'm back on the market. (Broads, the line starts here : ______________,
________________, ____________. Include name, phone, measurements) Now, she's all depressed
crying every night, calling him on the phone, begging, asking him "why". Typical sort of dejected
humiliated girl stuff, WOW, yeah, nothing like the misery of others to get the dopamine levels
in my brain to ticking, 'tain't no better drug than that. I mean, she's almost suicidal with it.
And it gives me a great thrill when rejected chicks get all "chicka boom" right. Like, "I'm an
independent strong woman." Yeah, right, now you are. Where was that shit before when you were
licking the soles of that limp prick's Steve Madden loafers? That's a totally ameobic and
reactive attitude. Just be honest, it's a coping tactic. So, now I'm pretty sure she's in love
with me. Cause every time I see her, there's like this clumsiness and unspoken tension. Like
this one time, we were in the car stuck in traffic on Irving Park, next to the cemetery, and I'm
screaming at Charlie, cause he's a fucking idiot and got on Irving right before a cubs game.
And she was totally there in the back seat, she was like, "yeah, Charlie, you suck." She wants me.
And you know I think back on all those nights that I caught a glimpse of her burning oceanic eyes
digging rivers into mine from underneath a veil of her sweeping ruby bangs, the soft outline of her
mouth illuminated by smoky light, refracted through sweet amber Whisky trickling over her teeth and
tongue. HOT DAMN, she wants to taste my lips. She's the kind of beautiful person that you
can hang around while wearing just a sock and she'd understand. She'd feel completely okay
with that, cause she knows that it's not some sort of joke, or a plea for attention, it's just
what you need to be at that moment. She'd respect the freedom of that sock, and the two of you
could cook Spanish dinner, and dance to Paul Simon on the roof. You'd watch her fall asleep
letting tulip pedals roll from the tips of your fingers, crashing across her ivory curves, while
you whisper Nick Drake lyrics in your head. Come the morning, she'd make you wrestle her
Greco-roman for your breakfast. But, the only time I ever see her is when she's with the band
and all we can do is speak all of our dreams and desires to each other with the fumbling of a
straw, or a glance that lasts one second too long. All the while, those vegan fecal freaks
don't even know that she's madly in love with me. What a horny situation.

Biography of sally by C. Mitchell Turner

The group sprung out of the collaborations of notedly prolific singer/songwriter Charlie Deets.
Almost born into music, his mother had worked in the rich Chicago radio scene during the hay-day
of musical exploration, and dated many of rock's greatest: Keith Richards, Mick Jagger, Paul Simon,
and J.C. Hooke of the Cryan' Shames. It is highly unlikely that Charlie was spawned from any of
these suitors. Instead, he was born the son of a printer.

As an only child, most of Charlie's youth was spent playing alone. It was only natural that he'd find
freedom and expression through music. A self taught guitarist, he had knocked around in many bands,
fitting in none. While away at college in Bloomington, Illinois, Charlie found himself fronting the
experimental pop fusion outfit, the borderlinejet. His popularity caught the eye of art student named
Melissa Neis and within a few months the two found themselves spending all there time together,
talking to no one else.

Missy was the sort of child that only a mother could love. The messy girl with tangled hair, wiping mud
and snot on her frilly expensive dress; the sort of girl you wouldn't want to have touching anything
of yours. Maybe she just had a different view of life, but her rebellious filth was just the sort of
escape that fulfilled the neurotic and frightened Charlie.

Sadly, the borderlinejet's, first ep failed miserably, and the band's popularity slowly faded from
central Illinois. Charlie's pre-existing fears from childhood spiraled out of control and consequentially
his grievous abuse of tranquilizers did as well. Strung out, paranoid, and angry, Charlie blamed
everyone around him for his failures. The band split, and so did the university and Charlie.
Broken, he quit making his music entirely and returned to Chicago only to find himself immersed in
his old lifestyle with old friends with self-destructive problems of their own.

Unwilling to sink into the gutter he packed up his worldly belongings and moved to Los Angeles.
To make ends meet Charlie worked in a recording studio that primary dealt in gangsta rap artists.
A life-changing event occurred in late April of '00. After an artistic disagreement in the studio,
a notorious producer held a firearm to Charlie's temple, handed him an acoustic guitar and told
him to play. What happened next could only be called divine intervention. Pilled out on Xanax,
Charlie calmly slid the guitar to the floor and shattered it with his foot, fortunately for him the
pistol was not loaded, and his job as an engineer came to a close.

With nowhere to turn he locked himself in his white one room apartment above Hollywood Blvd. With a
pawn shop guitar, a refrigerator full of Lunchables, and some Christmas lights, the next three months
became a lonely blur. The only musical output to come from his time in L.A. is the enigmatic
Peter Piper L.P., a collection of forgettable and opiated guitar driven drone songs with the occasional
scream or howl from Charlie. It also featured snippets of spoken word from the personal micro-cassette
taped ramblings of the controversial Actor/Philosopher Ben Dixon.

On a positive note, Missy had never given up hope on Charlie. She waited for him back in Illinois,
unwilling to give up the dream that one day the two of them could be happy together. But, like all things
in this world, nothing turns out as planned. Her psychotic babbling and endless drinking had become far
too much for Charlie, who had returned penniless to Chicago, only to take up residence on the floor of
alcoholic Screenwriter C. Mitchell Turner's apartment. The sort of life lived there proved far too much
of a strain on his frail mental state and Missy was at her wits end with the constant unfaithfulness,
the paranoid delusions, and the cavalcade of ruined teenage girls who surrounded Charlie's debouched life.
Finally, after a sickening visit from Missy, a distant Charlie notified Missy to never speak to him again.
The destructive bout had not only left Charlie homeless, but loveless as well. Finally, after years of madness,
Charlie sought help and two and a half years slipped by.

  1. With plenty of free time, Charlie had started writing song with astonishing intensity. He created
    two full length cds, the Flagrent Foul L.P. and the Dilated L.P. by himself, giving
    copies to his family and friends. He was extremely nervous about playing in public, but as
    attention grew towards his songwriting, he started to feel comfortable with music again.

Ready to play out, Charlie searched out a new band. Coming up empty handed around the scene,
Charlie was becoming disillusioned. It was a strange choice then, when after years of silence,
Charlie invited Missy to play music with him. Being an amateur guitar player, and naturally
adept at plucking at things, Missy picked up bass quickly. The two both understood that there
would be no love between them ever again.

Charlie knew all too well that he needed to make music; it was the only thing that gave him purpose.
If he was going to keep his sanity in this confusing world, than he had to follow his heart.
The band began to search out a drummer. Fortunately, C. Mitchell felt remorse about throwing
Charlie out, so as a consolation, he introduced him to his cousin.

Jeff Shafar, an amateur snowboarder/longboarder/photographer, was known for his endless reserves
of energy and the uncanny ability to do anything. Jeff started playing drums with the band and
the connection was made immediately. It is necessary to mention that showbiz is nothing new for Jeff,
he has danced in the Nutcracker for 12 consecutive years with the Salt Creek Ballet. He's also
performed in countless other productions from New York to Seattle. Swan Lake, Aaron Copeland's
Billy the Kid, and Stravinsky's Firebird Suite, just to name a few.

The band played out for the better half of 2003, but not without much trouble. It was not
uncommon to have a band member missing from a show. Charlie grew more and more increasingly
perturbed with the situation. The band decided to commit some music to record, and in
October of 2003, the band entered Gravity Studios to record with former skateboarder
Doug McBride at the helm. Paribus Records was the label kind enough to sponsor the endeavor.

And with that, this is sally, with their first lp, sally.
